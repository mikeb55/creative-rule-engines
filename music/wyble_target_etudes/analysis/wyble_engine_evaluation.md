## Conclusions

1. **Narrative Drift Etude C** is the strongest of the three: it has a clear motif, moving bass, and effective two-voice counterpoint. It qualifies as musical counterpoint with room for refinement.

2. **Shifting Lines Etude A** and **Northern Thread Etude A** behave as technical exercises. They demonstrate contrary motion and harmonic outline but lack melodic narrative, phrase structure, and variety. Both would benefit from the minimal rewrites suggested above.

3. **Common weaknesses across all three:**
   - Melody often ends in bass range (C#4, G4) instead of resolving in the upper voice
   - Bass tends toward arpeggio patterns; chromatic approaches are rare
   - Exact or near-exact repetition (Northern Thread, Narrative Drift) reduces musical interest

4. **Engine recommendation:** Prioritise Narrative Drift Etude C as the model. For Shifting Lines and Northern Thread, apply the minimal rewrites (cadence resolution, bass voice-leading, phrase variation) to shift from exercise to musical counterpoint.
