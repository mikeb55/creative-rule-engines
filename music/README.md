# 🎼 Creative Rule Engines – Music

This folder contains all **music-related rule engines, palettes, and templates** for your compositional ecosystem.

It is the *canonical source of truth* for:

- **Universal Composition Principles** (`composition-principles.md`) — Quality guidelines for all styles
- Criteria of Excellence for music
- Orchestral & chamber composition engines
- Jazz big band & hybrid large-ensemble engines
- Guitar trio & hybrid-guitar-chamber engines
- Songwriting form & harmony engines
- Jazz-specific references (`jazz-specific/`)
- Engraving rules (Sibelius-oriented)
- Harmonic palettes & Tonality Vault modules
- Lead sheet & orchestration templates

All Cursor music projects should reference this repo rather than redefining rules from scratch.

## Universal Principles

**`composition-principles.md`** contains universal composition principles that apply across all styles:
- Three-Level System (learning framework)
- MVP Framework (performance readiness)
- Practice Philosophy
- Development & Form
- Density & Space Management

These principles are **guidelines for quality**, not restrictive rules. They support good composition while allowing experimentation.

- **Orchestral:** See `orchestral-engine.md` (references universal principles)
