# Wyble Etudes — Quiet Orbit

**Source tune:** Quiet Orbit (V5.0)  
**Test:** test_frisell_counterpoint  
**Key:** D major | **Meter:** 4/4

---

## Section Inspiration

All three etudes derive from the **A section** (bars 1–8) of Quiet Orbit:  
Dmaj9 | Dmaj9 | Gmaj7 | Em7 | Am9 | Dmaj9 | Gmaj7 | Dmaj9

The wide intervallic spacing (B4–D6) and sustained tones from the tune inform the two-voice material. Rests and breathing space are preserved.

---

## Etude 1 — Contrary Motion Dyads

**Concept:** Upper and lower voices move in opposite directions. Dyads pair melodic fragments (F#, A, D, B–D6) with stepwise bass.

**Practice suggestions:**
- Honour the rests; they are part of the Frisell aesthetic.
- Play each voice separately; the lower voice outlines roots and chord tones.
- Practice the wide B4–D6 spans; use positions that allow clean voice separation.
- Use a slow tempo (♩ = 40–50) for the floating quality.

---

## Etude 2 — Voice Crossing and Interval Expansion

**Concept:** Voices may cross; intervals expand to reflect the tune’s wide spacing.

**Practice suggestions:**
- Identify voice crossings (e.g. B4 in lower voice vs D6 in upper).
- Work on interval expansion: 3rd → 6th → 10th in D major.
- Keep both voices audible when they cross.
- Use fingerstyle; let the sustain ring between gestures.

---

## Etude 3 — Moving Bass with Melodic Upper Voice

**Concept:** Active stepwise bass supports a sustained, lyrical upper melody.

**Practice suggestions:**
- Learn the bass line first; it should move through roots and chord tones.
- Add the melody above; the upper voice carries the tune’s sustained tones.
- Practice with a metronome; allow space between phrases.
- Aim for a singing, floating quality in the upper voice.

---

## General Guitar Practice

- **Frisell aesthetic:** Less is more; sustain and rests matter.
- **Fingerings:** Choose positions that allow clean voice separation.
- **Dynamics:** Use *p* to *mp* for a contemplative quality.
