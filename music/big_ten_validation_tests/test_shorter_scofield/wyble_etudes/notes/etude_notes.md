# Wyble Etudes — Narrative Drift

**Source tune:** Narrative Drift (V5.0)  
**Test:** test_shorter_scofield  
**Key:** C minor | **Meter:** 4/4

---

## Section Inspiration

All three etudes derive from the **A section** (bars 1–8) of Narrative Drift:  
Cm9 | Abmaj7 | Bb7 | Ebmaj7 | Cm9 | Abmaj7 | Bb7 | Ebmaj7

The opening melodic motif (Eb–G–Bb–D–Eb) and descending bass implication (C–Bb–A–G) inform the two-voice material.

---

## Etude 1 — Contrary Motion Dyads

**Concept:** Upper and lower voices move in opposite directions. Each dyad pairs a melodic note (from the tune) with a stepwise bass note outlining the chord.

**Practice suggestions:**
- Play each voice separately before combining.
- Focus on clean voice separation; avoid letting one hand dominate.
- Use a slow tempo (♩ = 60–80) until both lines feel independent.
- Emphasise the Eb–G–Bb–D–Eb motif in the upper voice.

---

## Etude 2 — Voice Crossing and Interval Expansion

**Concept:** Voices may cross (e.g. upper voice dips below lower). Intervals expand from thirds/sixths to wider spans (octaves, tenths).

**Practice suggestions:**
- Mark voice crossings in the score; practice those spots in isolation.
- Work on interval expansion: start with 3rds, then 6ths, then 8ves.
- Keep both voices audible when they cross; avoid accenting only the top line.
- Use alternate picking or fingerstyle to maintain clarity.

---

## Etude 3 — Moving Bass with Melodic Upper Voice

**Concept:** Active stepwise bass (root–7th–3rd–5th) supports a sustained or lyrical upper melody.

**Practice suggestions:**
- Learn the bass line first; it should feel like a walking bass.
- Add the melody above, ensuring each bass note is clearly articulated.
- Practice with a metronome; the bass should move on every beat or half.
- Aim for a singing quality in the upper voice.

---

## General Guitar Practice

- **Fingerings:** Experiment with positions that keep voice leading smooth.
- **Dynamics:** Try *p* for one voice and *mf* for the other to hear independence.
- **Tempo:** Start at half tempo; increase only when both voices are clean.
