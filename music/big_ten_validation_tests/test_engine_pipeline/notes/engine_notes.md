## Compositional Approach

### Structure (Wayne Shorter Narrative)
- **Form:** AABA, 32 bars
- **Motivic opening cell:** F4–A4–C5–E5–D5 (Dm9 ascending with 9th, resolving to root)
- **Development:** Motif returns at bars 1, 9, 17, 29 with variation (bar 9 ends on F5 instead of D5)

### Behaviour (Scofield–Holland Groove)
- **Tempo:** ♩ = 120 (medium swing)
- **Rhythmic lift:** Eighth-note motif cells, quarter/half responses, rests for groove punctuation
- **Active bass implication:** Root progression D → Bb → C → F (A); Gb → B → E → A (B) supports harmonic motion
- **Dyadic phrasing:** Two-note cells in melody (F–A, C–E, F–D) suggest guitar dyadic voicings

- **No technical exercise:** Melody remains primary; Wyble influence is colour, not dominant
