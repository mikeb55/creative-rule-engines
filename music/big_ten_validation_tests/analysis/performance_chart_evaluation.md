## Methodology

These are **single-line lead sheets** with chord symbols. For this evaluation:

- **Melody** = the notated pitch line
- **Bass** = implied by chord roots (what bass/comping would play from the symbols)
- **Counterpoint** = melody vs implied bass; whether the melody implies two-voice dialogue via register alternation

## Pieces Below GCE 9.0: Minimal Revision Suggestions

All four pieces score below 9.0. The following revisions are **minimal** and aimed at raising each toward or above 9.0 without changing the compositions.

1. **Add one phrase slur** on the Abmaj7 bar (2, 6, 18, 30) to reinforce phrase grouping (e.g. G5–C5–Eb4–G4 as a unit).
2. **Bar 12:** Add a brief performance cue such as "breathe" or "phrase end" before the whole-note Eb5 to clarify cadence.
3. **Engine cue:** Consider adding "motif returns" at bar 29 to reinforce narrative reprise.

### Shifting Lines (8.5 → 9.0+)

2. **Groove cue:** Add "6/8 feel" or "two-in-a-bar" at the top if the groove is ambiguous for players.
3. **Bridge bar 14:** Add "with lift" or "open" to support the E5–G#5–C#4 phrase peak.

### Quiet Orbit (8.3 → 9.0+)

2. **Bars 2, 10, 14, 18, 26 (full rest):** Add "breathe" or "space" to reinforce Frisell atmosphere.
3. **Bridge bar 13:** Add "floating" or "suspended" to support the Bb5 sustained tone.

### Northern Thread (8.8 → 9.0+)

1. **All four V7.0 performance charts function as musical counterpoint (A)**, not technical exercises. They support rehearsal and performance with clear style cues, rehearsal marks, and bridge labels.

2. **Wyble engine impact** is strongest in **Shifting Lines** (implied two-voice dialogue, register alternation) and present in **Northern Thread** (dyadic motion) and **Quiet Orbit** (spacious intervals). **Narrative Drift** emphasises Scofield groove over explicit counterpoint but maintains melodic/bass interaction.

3. **GCE range:** 8.3–8.8. All pieces are close to 9.0. The suggested revisions are minimal (cues, markings, one optional slur) and should raise scores without altering the compositions.

4. **Ensemble playability** is high across all four; the V7.0 performance chart pass successfully improved rehearsal usability.
