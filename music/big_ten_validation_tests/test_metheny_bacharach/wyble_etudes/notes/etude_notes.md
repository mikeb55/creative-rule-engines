# Wyble Etudes — Northern Thread

**Source tune:** Northern Thread (V5.0)  
**Test:** test_metheny_bacharach  
**Key:** Eb major | **Meter:** 4/4

---

## Section Inspiration

All three etudes derive from the **A section** (bars 1–8) of Northern Thread:  
Ebmaj7 | Cm7 | Fm7 | Bb7 | Ebmaj7 | Cm7 | Fm7 | Bb7

The lyrical motif (G–Bb–C) and elegant voice-leading from the tune inform the two-voice material.

---

## Etude 1 — Contrary Motion Dyads

**Concept:** Upper and lower voices move in opposite directions. Each dyad pairs a melodic note (from the G–Bb–C motif) with a stepwise bass note.

**Practice suggestions:**
- Play each voice separately before combining.
- Focus on the G–Bb–C motif in the upper voice.
- Use a slow tempo (♩ = 70–84) for lyrical phrasing.
- Emphasise clean voice separation; avoid block chords.

---

## Etude 2 — Voice Crossing and Interval Expansion

**Concept:** Voices may cross; intervals expand from thirds to sixths and tenths.

**Practice suggestions:**
- Mark voice crossings in the score; practice those spots in isolation.
- Work on interval expansion in Eb major context.
- Keep both voices audible when they cross.
- Use fingerstyle for a singing quality.

---

## Etude 3 — Moving Bass with Melodic Upper Voice

**Concept:** Active stepwise bass (root–7th–3rd–5th) supports a lyrical upper melody.

**Practice suggestions:**
- Learn the bass line first; it should outline the chord progression.
- Add the melody above; the upper voice carries the tune’s lyrical arc.
- Practice with a metronome; the bass should move on every beat or half.
- Aim for a Metheny/Bacharach singing quality in the upper voice.

---

## General Guitar Practice

- **Lyrical phrasing:** Legato and sustain matter; avoid choppy articulation.
- **Fingerings:** Choose positions that allow smooth voice leading.
- **Dynamics:** Use *mp* to *mf* for expressive warmth.
