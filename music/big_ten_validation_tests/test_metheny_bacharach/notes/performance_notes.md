# Performance Notes: Northern Thread

## Style Feel

Lyrical Metheny/Bacharach contour with elegant dyadic motion. Lyrical, sing the line. ♩ = 84. Eb major, AABA.

## Rehearsal Suggestions

- Sing the melody before playing; the long arcs (e.g. G5–Bb–C6) should feel vocal.
- Rehearse the bridge (B) separately—Fmaj7, Bmaj7 introduce a Bacharach-style harmonic surprise.
- At A3, cue “Back to lyrical flow” so the return feels natural and song-like.
- Phrase slurs mark the main melodic arcs; honour them as breathing points.

## What the Band Should Listen For

- Long melodic arcs as the primary lyrical element.
- The bridge’s harmonic surprise (Fmaj7, Bmaj7) as a distinct colour change.
- Elegant voice-leading and dyadic motion in the melody.

## Where the Bridge Changes Character

**Bars 13–16 (B):** The bridge introduces Fmaj7, Bmaj7, Gm7, C7. Harmonic colour shifts from Eb major to a brighter, more open palette. The chart marks this as “Bridge — colour change · with lift.” At bar 17 (A3), the cue “Back to lyrical flow” signals the return to the main lyrical contour.
