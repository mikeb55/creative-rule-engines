# Double Time Excellence (DTE) Framework v2.2

## Purpose

Evaluation and generation framework for jazz double-time line generation, critique, scoring, and MusicXML output.

**Scope:** Applies to all jazz double-time material within jazz swing grammar.

---

## Core Definition

**Double Time (DT)** is defined as **perceptual velocity** created by **note density or harmonic event density** **within a jazz swing grammar**.

**Critical:** Speed alone does not qualify as DT. DT requires swing grammar and harmonic context.

---

## Mandatory Evaluation Framework

All DT material must be evaluated using:

### Global DTE Score (0–10)

Overall assessment of double-time excellence.

### 10 Sub-Rules (0–10 each)

1. **Time feel & pulse lock**
   - Does the DT lock with the rhythm section?
   - Is the pulse clear and consistent?

2. **Rhythmic accuracy & subdivision**
   - Are subdivisions accurate?
   - Does the rhythm serve the groove?

3. **Making the changes** *(audible resolutions required)*
   - Can the harmony be sung back?
   - Are resolution points (3rds, 7ths) audible?
   - Judged by **audible resolution points**, not scale choice.

4. **Bebop language & chromaticism** *(syntactic correctness)*
   - Uses grammatical enclosures and resolutions
   - Not decorative chromaticism
   - Parker *sentences* score higher than Parker *fragments*

5. **Motivic development & rhetoric** *(sentences > runs)*
   - In DT contexts, **rhetoric outranks density**
   - Short, complete phrases score higher than long continuous runs
   - Sentences and phrases > mechanical runs

6. **Space & contrast**
   - Does the DT earn its contrast?
   - Is space used effectively?

7. **Phrase contour**
   - Does the line have shape and direction?
   - Is the contour musical?

8. **Articulation, swing & dynamics**
   - Is the swing feel present?
   - Are articulations appropriate?

9. **Technical clarity & playability**
   - Is the material physically playable?
   - Does it serve the music technically?

10. **Singability & personal voice**
    - Can the line be sung?
    - Does it have character and voice?

---

## Critical Rule Updates (v2.2)

### Rule 3 – Making the Changes

- Judged by **audible resolution points** (3rds, 7ths), not scale choice
- If the harmony cannot be sung back, the changes were not made
- Harmonic clarity under velocity is essential

### Rule 4 – Bebop Language

- Bebop DT must use **grammatical enclosures and resolutions**, not decorative chromaticism
- Parker *sentences* score higher than Parker *fragments*
- Syntactic correctness matters more than note density

### Rule 5 – Motivic Development

- In DT contexts, **rhetoric outranks density**
- Short, complete phrases score higher than long continuous runs
- Musical sentences > mechanical sequences

---

## DT Exclusion & Boundary Clause

Mark **DT = N/A by design** (do not score) when:

- Speed uses **block-chord velocity** (different mechanism)
- Subdivision is **straight (fusion/rock)** (different grammar)
- DT is **deliberately avoided** as an aesthetic choice (e.g., Wes Montgomery style)

**No penalty for DT avoidance.** DT is a tool, not a requirement.

---

## Valid DT Forms (Explicitly Allowed)

- **Single-note linear DT** (most common)
- **Dyad-based DT** (two-note intervals)
- **Two-voice contrapuntal DT** (Jimmy Wyble lineage)

**Note:** DT may be driven by **harmonic motion**, not just note speed.

## Stylistic Anchors

### Charlie Parker
- Bebop DT syntax benchmark
- Swing + functional harmony
- Sentence structure and rhetoric

### Pat Martino
- Linear DT engine
- Harmonic clarity under velocity

### Jimmy Wyble
- Chordal / contrapuntal DT authority
- Two-voice DT approach

---

## Default Test Context

- **Primary DT test-bed:** *Angular Motion*
- Other tunes require explicit approval to host DT

---

## DT Dosage Rule (3-Chorus Solos)

- **Total DT:** ~25–30% of solo
- **Back-loaded** (appears later in solo)
- **Never 100% of a chorus**
- DT must earn contrast through space and pacing

---

## Authority Principle

> DT excellence is defined by **clarity, rhetoric, and resolution under velocity**,
> not by speed, density, or endurance.

**Quality over quantity. Clarity over density. Rhetoric over runs.**

---

## Application Guidelines

### For Generation
- Apply DTE framework when generating DT material
- Use stylistic anchors as reference points
- Ensure all 10 sub-rules are considered

### For Critique
- Evaluate existing DT material using the 10-point framework
- Provide specific feedback on each sub-rule
- Identify areas for improvement

### For Scoring
- Use the global DTE score (0–10) for overall assessment
- Break down scores by sub-rule when providing detailed feedback
- Mark DT = N/A when appropriate (see Exclusion Clause)
