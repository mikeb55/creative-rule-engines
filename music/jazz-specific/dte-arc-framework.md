# DTE-ARC Framework (Double Time Excellence - Arc Evaluation)
## Multi-Chorus Solo Evaluation

**Purpose:** Evaluation framework for multi-chorus jazz solos, especially 3-chorus solos, integrating DTE evaluation with structural arc assessment.

**Scope:** Applies to all multi-chorus solo generation, critique, scoring, and MusicXML output.

---

## Relationship to DTE Framework

**DTE Framework** (`dte-framework.md`) evaluates individual double-time sections.

**DTE-ARC Framework** evaluates the complete solo arc, including:
- Overall structure and development
- Chorus-by-chorus evaluation
- DT dosage and placement
- Integration of DTE scores for DT sections

**Note:** For DT sections within a solo, use the DTE framework (10 sub-rules). DTE-ARC evaluates how those sections fit into the overall arc.

---

## Three-Chorus Solo Structure

### Chorus 1: Establish
- **Purpose:** Establish motifs, space, inside playing
- **DT:** None (or minimal if stylistically appropriate)
- **Focus:** Clarity, motif presentation, harmonic foundation
- **Space:** Generous use of space and rests
- **Evaluation:** Motif clarity, harmonic awareness, space usage

### Chorus 2: Develop or Disrupt
- **Purpose:** Develop motifs, controlled intensity
- **DT:** Optional, but if present, should be controlled
- **Focus:** Motivic development, variation, controlled intensity
- **Space:** Moderate space, building intensity
- **Evaluation:** Motivic development, controlled intensity, DT quality (if present)

### Chorus 3: Peak and Resolve
- **Purpose:** Peak intensity, clear release
- **DT:** Most common location for DT (back-loaded)
- **Focus:** Earned intensity, clear resolution, release
- **Space:** Strategic space for contrast and release
- **Evaluation:** Intensity earned, DT quality (if present), clear resolution

---

## DT Dosage Rules (3-Chorus Solos)

- **Total DT:** ~25–30% of solo
- **Back-loaded** (appears later in solo, typically Chorus 3)
- **Never 100% of a chorus**
- **DT must earn contrast** through space and pacing

**Exception:** A solo may pass with DT = N/A if stylistically appropriate (e.g., Wes Montgomery style, ballad approach).

## DTE-ARC Evaluation Framework

### Overall Solo Score (0–10)

Assessment of the complete solo arc, considering structure, development, and DT integration.

### Chorus-by-Chorus Evaluation

#### Chorus 1 Score (0–10)
- Motif clarity and presentation
- Harmonic awareness
- Space usage
- Foundation for development

#### Chorus 2 Score (0–10)
- Motivic development
- Controlled intensity
- Variation and transformation
- DT quality (if present) — use DTE framework

#### Chorus 3 Score (0–10)
- Intensity earned
- DT quality (if present) — use DTE framework
- Clear resolution
- Release and closure

### DT Section Evaluation

For any DT sections within the solo:
- **Use DTE Framework** (10 sub-rules, 0–10 each)
- Evaluate DT sections separately
- Integrate DT scores into overall arc assessment

### Structural Arc Assessment

- **Development:** Does the solo evolve over 3 choruses?
- **Contrast:** Is contrast earned through space and pacing?
- **Resolution:** Is there clear resolution and release?
- **Coherence:** Does the solo feel like a complete statement?

---

## Integration with DTE Framework

When evaluating a multi-chorus solo:

1. **Evaluate overall arc** using DTE-ARC framework
2. **Evaluate DT sections** using DTE framework (10 sub-rules)
3. **Integrate scores:** Overall arc score + DT section scores = Complete assessment

**Example:**
- Overall Arc: 8/10
- Chorus 1: 8/10 (establishment)
- Chorus 2: 7/10 (development)
- Chorus 3: 9/10 (peak + resolution)
- DT Section (C3 Bridge): DTE 8.5/10
- **Final Assessment:** Strong solo with excellent DT integration

---

## Authority Principle

> Multi-chorus solo excellence is defined by **structural integrity, developmental arc, and integration of DT sections**,
> not by constant intensity, mechanical runs, or DT for its own sake.

**Structure over density. Development over repetition. Integration over isolation.**
