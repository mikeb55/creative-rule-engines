### Three-Level System (Jazz)
- **Level 1:** Jazz harmony vocabulary (chord symbols, extensions, scales)
- **Level 2:** Physical voicings (shell voicings, drop-2, quartal)
- **Level 3:** Harmonic navigation (voice-leading, form awareness)

### MVG Framework (Jazz)
- **Fast Path:** Head + basic voicings + one solo chorus
- **Vocabulary Builder:** Scale palettes + triad pairs + all choruses
- **Study Mode:** Harmonic analysis + form study + listening
