# 3. The 12-Etude System

Each tune receives two etudes.

6 tunes × 2 etudes = 12 etudes total.

Etudes are 4–8 bars and loopable.

## Etude Type 1 – Guide Tone Line

Purpose:
Internalise harmonic movement.

Play continuous 3rd–7th voice-leading lines through the form.

Example concept:

Fmaj7 → A  
Ebmaj7 → G  
Dbmaj7 → F

---

## Etude Type 2 – Bass + Dyad Study

Purpose:
Simulate ensemble comping.

Practice:

root note in bass + upper dyad.

Inspired by:

Jimmy Wyble  
Ed Bickert  
Jim Hall
