# Ensemble Rules

Rules for ensemble writing across chamber, big band, and orchestral contexts.

## Who Has the Line

- Always know: **who has the line**, who shadows, who supports
- One primary melody at a time unless polyphonic engine
- Avoid muddy mid-low blocks

## Section Balance

- Design contrast: solo → chamber subset → tutti
- Dynamic arcs over full sections, not bar-by-bar flickering
- Brass: weight, affect, fatigue awareness
- Winds: breathing, range limits, idiomatic intervals
- Strings: bowings, crossings, divisi clarity

## Guitar-Specific

- Drop-2/3, 3-note voicings, no piano clusters
- Dyadic voicings for comping when appropriate
- Idiomatic range and voicing spread

## Orchestration Consciousness

- Avoid doubling that obscures clarity
- Know register distribution
- Leave space for soloists
