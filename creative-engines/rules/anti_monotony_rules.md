# Anti-Monotony Rules

Rules to prevent repetitive, generic, or robotic composition.

## Motivic Variation

- Recur motifs every 4–8 bars with transformation (inversion, sequence, augmentation, diminution, retrograde, fragmentation)
- Avoid literal repetition for more than 2 bars without variation
- Vary register, rhythm, or harmony on returns

## Harmonic Avoidance

- No more than 2 consecutive bars of identical chord voicing
- Vary tension level (L1 → L2 → L3) across sections
- Use polychords, triad pairs, or interval cycles to break predictability

## Rhythmic Avoidance

- Vary phrase lengths (avoid constant 4-bar blocks)
- Use displacement, syncopation, or metric modulation
- Avoid robotic eighth-note streams without accent variation

## Textural Avoidance

- Contrast density: solo → chamber subset → tutti
- Vary who has the line across sections
- Avoid same orchestration for more than 8 bars

## "AI Smell" Checklist

- ❌ Generic patterns
- ❌ Robotic lines
- ❌ Filler bars
- ❌ Underdeveloped movements
- ❌ Predictable resolutions
