# Engine Rotation Examples

Examples of how to rotate or combine engines across a project.

## Single-Engine Rotation

Use one engine per piece, rotating through the palette:

| Piece | Engine |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Scofield-Holland |
| 2 | Shorter Narrative |
| 3 | Frisell Atmosphere |
| 4 | Wheeler Lyric |
| 5 | Stravinsky Pulse |
| 6 | Zappa Disruption |
| 7 | Slonimsky Harmonic |
| 8 | Bartók Night |
| 9 | Counterpoint Hybrid |
| 10 | Polyphonic Labyrinth |

## Album Balance

- **Groove** (Scofield): 1–2 pieces
- **Narrative** (Shorter): 1–2 pieces
- **Atmospheric** (Frisell, Long Echo): 1–2 pieces
- **Lyrical** (Wheeler): 1–2 pieces
- **Rhythmic** (Stravinsky): 0–1 pieces
- **Disruptive** (Zappa): 0–1 pieces
- **Harmonic** (Slonimsky): 0–1 pieces
- **Dark** (Bartók): 0–1 pieces
- **Contrapuntal** (Counterpoint, Polyphonic): 1–2 pieces

## Hybrid Combinations

- **Counterpoint + Tonality:** Hybrid chamber with lyric gravity
- **Frisell + Wheeler:** Sparse but lyrical
- **Shorter + Zappa:** Narrative with structural disruption
- **Scofield + Slonimsky:** Groove with systematic harmony

## Avoid

- Too many groove-heavy pieces in a row
- Too many dense polyphonic pieces without contrast
- Loss of engine identity when mixing
