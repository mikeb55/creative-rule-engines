# Polychords

One triad or chord over another.

## Examples

- C in bass + D major triad → Cmaj7(9,#11) colour
- Abmaj7(#11)/C → warm, rich subdominant colour in C
- F#m11 over D bass → Dmaj9(#11) feel

## Usage

- Notation: "Abmaj7(#11)/C" or polychord stacks in analysis
- Assign lower structure to low instruments, upper structure to brighter instruments
