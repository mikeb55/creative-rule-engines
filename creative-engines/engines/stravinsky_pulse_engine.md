# Stravinsky Pulse Engine

## Core Priorities

- **Pulse** — Rhythmic drive, metric displacement
- **Ostinato** — Repetition with variation
- **Metric irregularity** — Shifting accents, polyrhythm
- **Orchestral colour** — Block scoring, timbral contrast

## Characteristics

- Strong rhythmic identity
- Ostinato-based development
- Metric modulation potential
- Block-like orchestration
- Primitivist / neoclassical energy

## Avoid

- Static metre without displacement
- Predictable phrase lengths
- Loss of rhythmic drive
- Over-smooth transitions
