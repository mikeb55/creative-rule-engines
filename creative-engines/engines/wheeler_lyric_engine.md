# Wheeler Lyric Engine

## Core Priorities

- **Melody** — Singable, protected, central
- **Warmth** — Lyrical arc, emotional gravity
- **Harmonic surprise** — Unexpected modulations (bII, bVI, major lift)
- **Lydian / bitonal** — Jazz-classical blend

## Characteristics

- Warm voicings
- Clear lyrical arc
- Bacharach-style harmonic surprise
- Flugelhorn/brass for lyrical statements
- Avoid overcomplication

## Avoid

- Over-complication of the tune
- Generic harmonic progressions
- Loss of melodic centrality
- Cold or clinical voicings
