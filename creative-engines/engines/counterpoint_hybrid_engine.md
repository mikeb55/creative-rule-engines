# Counterpoint Hybrid Engine

## Core Priorities

- **Chamber voicings** — Clear, independent lines
- **Counterpoint** — Two or more independent voices
- **Lyric gravity** — Emotional pacing, weight
- **Tonality Vault** — Harmonic depth with contrapuntal logic

## Characteristics

- Two-line or multi-line logic
- Independence of voices (no block harmony)
- Guitar + strings or guitar duo
- Motivic development across voices
- Avoid groove-heavy reinterpretations unless justified

## Avoid

- Block harmony
- Doubling that obscures independence
- Loss of contrapuntal clarity
- Over-dense textures
