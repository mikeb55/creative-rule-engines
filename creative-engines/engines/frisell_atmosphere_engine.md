# Frisell Atmosphere Engine

## Core Priorities

- **Space** — Breathable texture, room between attacks
- **Colour** — Sparse voicings, suspended harmony
- **Restraint** — Minimal activity, long-held notes
- **Suspended harmony** — Avoid resolution pressure

## Characteristics

- Slow tempo
- Dyadic guitar (avoid full triads)
- Long-held pads, sustained tones
- Atmospheric decay, gradual entry
- Melody emerges from stillness

## Avoid

- Dense voicings
- Rhythmic fills
- Over-complication
- Loss of breathability

Part of the **Mighty Ten** global composition framework. See `creative-engines/docs/engine_master_palette.md`.
