# Wayne Shorter Narrative Engine

## Core Priorities

- **Motivic storytelling** — Sections unfold like scenes
- **Sectional logic** — Clear divisions (A, B, bridge, etc.)
- **Narrative pacing** — Structural variation over simple reharmonisation
- **Motivic development** — Dramatic contrast between sections

## Characteristics

- Motivic cells and foreshadowing
- Harmonic ambiguity typical of Shorter
- Varied textures across sections
- Interludes and transitional moments
- Endings that resist closure (open vs closed options)

## Avoid

- Fixed forms and predictable grooves
- Over-resolved cadences
- Loss of motivic logic
- Generic harmonic progressions
