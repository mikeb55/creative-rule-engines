## Criterion Notes

### 1. Interval Cell Integrity

All 12 phrases trace pitches to their assigned cell. Cell A (m2→M3), B (m3→tritone), C (P4→m2), D (M2→tritone), E (m3→M2) are audible in the event sets.

### 2. Harmonic Ambiguity

All phrases sustain field-based harmony. No ii–V–I or dominant-tonic resolution. Fields A–D used as regions, not progressions.

### 3. Phrase Asymmetry

All phrase structures (3+5, 5+4, 4+4+3, 7+5, 3+3+2) are asymmetrical. No extended symmetric phrasing.

### 4. Rhythmic Layering

**PASS:** Phrases 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12 — clear pulse anchor or balanced displacement.

**WARN:** Phrases 3, 5, 9, 11 — dual off-beat layers (delayed_entry + pulse_displacement) without consistent base_pulse anchor. Risk of rhythmic drift toward "avant-jazz soup."

### 5. Ensemble Equality

All phrases distribute melody_fragment, counterline, and cluster_color. No bar functions as pure accompaniment.

### 6. Anti-Cadential Check

All phrases end on suspended or open material. No tonal resolution.

### 7. Hill Identity Stability

**PASS:** Phrases with base_pulse anchor or balanced stretch maintain strongest Hill character.

**WARN:** Phrases 3, 5, 9, 11 — when both rhythmic layers avoid the downbeat, identity weakens toward generic modern-jazz drift.

## Commentary

### Overall

Hill identity **survived** the stress test. All 12 phrases satisfy the core constraints: no tonal cadence, traceable cells, ambiguous fields, ensemble equality. The stress test exposed a **rhythmic layer vulnerability**: phrases that combine delayed_entry + pulse_displacement without a base_pulse anchor tend toward rhythmic diffuseness.

### Strongest Performers

- **Phrase 1** (Cell A, Field A, 3+5): Clean base_pulse + delayed_entry; 3+5 clearly articulated.
- **Phrase 4** (Cell D, Field D, 7+5): Dark Field D color; strong convergence at bar 7.
- **Phrase 10** (Cell E, Field B, 4+4+3): Balanced layers; 3+2+3 phrase groups distinct.

### Weakest Performers

- **Phrase 3** (Cell C, Field C, 4+4+3): Dual off-beat layers; no downbeat anchor.
- **Phrase 5** (Cell E, Field A, 3+3+2): Same issue; phrase_stretch + pulse_displacement both displace.
- **Phrase 11** (Cell A, Field C, 3+3+2): delayed_entry + pulse_displacement; rhythmic identity diffuse.

### No Tonal Implication

None of the 12 phrases implied functional tonality. Field-based harmony held across all variants.
