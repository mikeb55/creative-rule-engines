# Andrew Hill Phrase Generator

The phrase engine constructs asymmetric phrase groups.

## Allowed Phrase Structures

- 3 + 5
- 5 + 4
- 4 + 4 + 3
- 7 + 5

## Phrase Behavior

- motif restart inside phrase
- delayed cadence
- interruption before resolution

Reject phrase symmetry that repeats more than twice.
