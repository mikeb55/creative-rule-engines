# Andrew Hill Validation Checklist

## Pass Conditions

- output uses structured event schema
- phrase grouping is asymmetric
- interval cell identity remains audible in transformed form
- harmonic field remains ambiguous
- at least two rhythmic layers are active
- ensemble roles are distributed structurally
- no bar functions as pure accompaniment for the entire phrase

## Fail Conditions

- phrase becomes symmetrical 4+4
- harmony implies clear dominant-tonic cadence
- all rhythm aligns to one uniform layer
- counterline disappears
- cluster pressure is constant
- source interval cell becomes untraceable
