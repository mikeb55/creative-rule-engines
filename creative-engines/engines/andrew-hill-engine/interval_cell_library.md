# Andrew Hill Interval Cell Library

These cells form the melodic and harmonic vocabulary of the Hill engine.

Cells must be used for:

- melodic contour
- harmonic construction
- counterline generation

---

## CELL A

**minor 2 → major 3**

Example pitch sets:

- C Db E
- F Gb A

---

## CELL B

**minor 3 → tritone**

Example:

- C Eb A
- D F B

---

## CELL C

**perfect 4 → minor 2**

Example:

- C F Gb
- D G Ab

---

## CELL D

**major 2 → tritone**

Example:

- C D Ab
- E F# C

---

## CELL E

**minor 3 → major 2**

Example:

- C Eb F
- D F G

---

## CELL TRANSFORMATIONS

**Allowed transformations:**

- repeat fragment
- compress interval
- expand interval
- invert cell
- shift register
