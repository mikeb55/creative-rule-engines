# Andrew Hill Ambiguous Harmonic Fields

Harmonic fields represent tonal regions rather than functional harmony.

Fields are built from interval cells.

Avoid traditional tonal cadences.

---

## FIELD A

**C Eb F# G**

**Behavior:**

- dark cluster color
- unstable tonal center
- supports angular melody

---

## FIELD B

**D F G Bb**

**Behavior:**

- modal ambiguity
- supports displaced counterlines

---

## FIELD C

**E G Bb C#**

**Behavior:**

- high tension field
- cluster pivot potential

---

## FIELD D

**F Ab B C**

**Behavior:**

- chromatic cluster color
- dark register emphasis

---

## FIELD MOTION RULES

Harmonic motion occurs by:

- shared pitch pivot
- interval cell mutation
- register shift

Avoid dominant-tonic resolution.
