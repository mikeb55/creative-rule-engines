# Bartók Night Engine

## Core Priorities

- **Night textures** — Polymodal, quartal/quintal
- **Darkness** — Controlled instability, pressure
- **Folk-inflected** — Asymmetric rhythms, modal colour
- **Chamber density** — Clear but rich voicings

## Characteristics

- Polymodal harmony
- Quartal/quintal voicings
- Night-music atmosphere
- Asymmetric phrase lengths
- Tension and contrast

## Avoid

- Over-resolution
- Generic minor harmony
- Loss of folk character
- Predictable form
