# Wayne Shorter Style Grammar

**Purpose:** Define the compositional grammar for the Wayne Shorter Engine.

---

## 1. Interval Cell Logic

- All melodic material derives from interval cells (see shorter_interval_cell_library.md).
- Cells consist of 3–5 pitch classes; intervals: m2, M2, m3, M3, P4, tt.
- Cells may be used for: melodic contour, harmonic construction, counterline.
- No arbitrary scalar or arpeggiated material.

---

## 2. Motif Transformation Rules

| Transformation | Description | Constraint |
|----------------|-------------|------------|
| repeat | Exact or near-exact recurrence | Max 2 consecutive |
| invert | Mirror around axis | Axis = first or last note |
| transpose | Shift by interval | m3, tt, P4 preferred |
| fragment | Use subset of cell | Min 3 notes |
| extend | Add notes to cell | Max 6 notes |
| interrupt | Cut off; restart | Restart within 2 bars |

---

## 3. Harmonic Color Fields

- Modal: Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian.
- Chromatic planing: half-step, whole-step.
- Pedal: static bass, shifting upper structure.
- Suspended: resolution deferred; tension sustained.

See shorter_harmonic_fields.md for field definitions.

---

## 4. Chromatic Root Movement

**Allowed:**

- Half-step (up or down)
- Whole-step (up or down)
- Minor 3rd
- Tritone
- Perfect 4th

**Avoid:**

- Predictable ii–V–I
- Dominant-tonic as default
- Circle-of-fifths sequences

---

## 5. Non-Functional Harmony

- Root movement by color, not function.
- Resolution via: register change, density release, motivic return.
- Tritone substitution as structural.
- Chord extensions (9, #11, ♭5) as structural.

---

## 6. Phrase Asymmetry

**Allowed patterns:**

- 3+5, 5+4, 4+4+3, 7+5
- 3+3+2, 6+6, 5+7, 4+3+4
- Irregular: 3+4+5, 5+3+4, etc.

**Boundary behaviors:**

- Delayed cadence
- Early cadence
- Overlap
- Restart

**Reject:** Strict 4+4 for 8+ bars without variation.

---

## 7. Irregular Phrase Lengths

- Phrase lengths may be 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 bars (or beats).
- No default to 4-bar phrases.
- Phrases may "breathe" — extend or truncate.

---

## 8. Narrative Form Structures

- Form emerges from motivic development.
- Sections link without literal repetition.
- Build, release, suspend as narrative arc.
- Short complete forms (8–16 bars for themes).

---

## 9. Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

- Scalar bebop lines
- ii–V–I as default
- 4+4+4+4 symmetry throughout
- Melody + chord accompaniment only
- Generic jazz turnarounds
- Clichéd licks
