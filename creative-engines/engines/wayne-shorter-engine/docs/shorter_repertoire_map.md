## Repertoire Summary for Engine

### Primary Reference Compositions

1. **Footprints** — Blues with chromatic turnaround; cross-rhythm; bass pattern.
2. **Nefertiti** — Minimal harmony; melody-focused; space.
3. **Speak No Evil** — Motivic theme; two-horn; modal.
4. **Dance Cadaverous** — Off-kilter; atmospheric; asymmetric.
5. **Juju** — Quartet; urgent; motivic.
6. **Infant Eyes** — Ballad; suspended; lyrical.

### Form Archetypes to Model

- 12-bar blues with chromatic turnaround
- AABA with altered bridge
- Through-composed short form (2–4 min)
- Modal vamp with motivic development
- Irregular phrase chain (modular)

### Harmonic Archetypes

- Modal (Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian)
- Chromatic planing (half-step, whole-step)
- Pedal with shifting upper structure
- Suspended resolution
- Tritone substitution as structural
