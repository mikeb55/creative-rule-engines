# Wayne Shorter Rhythmic Patterns

Shorter rhythmic logic uses layered pulse perception and cross-rhythm.

Each phrase may include multiple rhythmic strata.

---

## PATTERN A — Base Pulse

Motif attacks on strong beats (1, 3 in 4/4; or downbeats).

---

## PATTERN B — Delayed Entry

Motif begins one eighth-note (or quarter) after expected attack.

---

## PATTERN C — Pulse Displacement

Motif repeats one beat later than original position.

---

## PATTERN D — Phrase Stretch

Last motif fragment extends beyond phrase boundary.

---

## PATTERN E — Tresillo (3+3+2)

Bass or accompaniment uses 3+3+2 grouping (Afro-Cuban).

Common in Footprints-style contexts.

---

## PATTERN F — 3-Over-2 Cross-Rhythm

Three pulses against two (or vice versa).

Metric ambiguity (6/4 vs 3/4 vs 4/4).

---

## PATTERN G — Syncopated Entry

Motif enters on off-beat (e.g., & of 2, & of 4).

---

## RHYTHMIC RULES

1. At least one rhythmic convergence point every 4–8 bars.
2. Reject rhythmic chaos without convergence.
3. Syncopation as structural, not decorative.
4. Cross-rhythm (tresillo, 3-over-2) optional but allowed.

---

## LAYER COMBINATIONS

**Allowed:**

- base_pulse + delayed_entry
- base_pulse + pulse_displacement
- base_pulse + phrase_stretch
- delayed_entry + phrase_stretch

**Require pulse anchor:** If using displacement layers, include ≥1 base_pulse event per 2-bar span.

**Forbidden (as sole pair):** delayed_entry + pulse_displacement without base_pulse elsewhere.

---

## CONVERGENCE

Convergence = moment when multiple rhythmic layers align on same beat.

For phrases ≥ 10 bars: convergence interval ≤ 6 bars.
