## FC01 — Asymmetrical Phrase Chain (3+5 → 4+4 → 7+5)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Form ID** | FC01 |
| **Total bars** | 24 |
| **Phrase lengths** | 3+5 (8) → 4+4 (8) → 7+5 (12) |
| **Motivic development** | Cell A (m3–tt) stated in phrase 1; inverted in phrase 2 (4+4 with harmonic field shift); fragmented + extended in phrase 3. Motif trace continuous across all three phrases. |
| **Harmonic field progression** | Field A (Dorian) → Field D (chromatic planing) → Field F (pedal). Three distinct field types. |
| **Ensemble role behavior** | Melody (Cell A), counterline (Cell A fragment, delayed entry), harmonic_color (Field voicings), bass (pedal in phrase 3). |
