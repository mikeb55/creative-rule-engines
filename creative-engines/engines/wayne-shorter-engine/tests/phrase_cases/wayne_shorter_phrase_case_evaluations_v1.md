## EVALUATION LEGEND

- **Ch1:** Motivic trace (≥70% trace; motif ≥2× transformed)
- **Ch2:** Harmonic ambiguity (≤1 V–I per 8 bars; planing/modal)
- **Ch3:** Phrase asymmetry (irregular grouping or 4+4 with asymmetry)
- **Ch4:** Interval logic consistency (cell-aligned; no scalar runs)
- **Ch5:** Harmonic color diversity (≥2 distinct types or transformation)
- **Ch6:** Loop/narrative (no literal repeat)
- **Ch7:** Monophonic collapse (≥2 roles)
- **Ch8:** GCE ≥ 9.0
